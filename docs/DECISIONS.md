# Decisions

One line each: decision, then why.

- **2026-09-28 · TypeScript 5.9 (not 7.x).** Stable, well-known compiler API; TS 7 is newly released. Revisit later.
- **2026-09-28 · Node 24 LTS installed via winget.** Nothing was installed; LTS is the safe default.
- **2026-09-28 · Crouch is bound to C only, not Ctrl.** Ctrl+W closes the tab and can't be blocked outside fullscreen.
- **2026-09-28 · Crouch lowers eyes (and later the hitbox) but not the movement capsule.** No crawl spaces in Phase 1; avoids stand-up-inside-geometry bugs.
- **2026-09-28 · Characters don't physically collide with each other in Rapier.** The sim will handle spacing and BB hits; keeps physics simple and deterministic.
- **2026-09-28 · Gravity 20 m/s², jump apex 0.68 m (measured), 0.55 s cooldown, 30 m/s terminal fall speed.** Snappier than real gravity; jump is a hop, not a traversal tool.
- **2026-09-28 · Walkable ledges ≤ 0.15 m.** Rapier autostep with a capsule only clears ~0.15 m reliably (measured); maps avoid ledges between that and jump height.
- **2026-09-28 · FOV is configured as 100° horizontal at 16:9, Hor+.** Three.js FOV is vertical; a fixed vertical FOV avoids fisheye on ultrawide while matching CS/Valorant-like width on 16:9.
- **2026-09-28 · Default sensitivity 0.001 rad per mouse count.** Close to the CS default (0.00096), a familiar baseline; slider 0.2×–4×, stored in localStorage.
- **2026-09-28 · Commands are keyed by character id (Map), not array index.** Bots joining/leaving can't misroute input.
- **2026-09-28 · Level materials are MeshLambert with merged geometry per texture.** Cheap on integrated GPUs; a few draw calls for the whole map.
- **2026-09-28 · Pixel ratio capped at 1.5, PCF shadows (2048 map).** High-DPI laptops with iGPUs can't afford native resolution at 60 FPS; PCFSoft was removed in three r186.
- **2026-09-28 · Rapier compat build (inlined WASM) in its own chunk: 4.3 MB raw / 1.7 MB gzip.** Simplest loading; well under the 30 MB budget. Three.js is also split out (0.5 MB / 0.13 MB gzip).
- **2026-09-28 · Dev-only `?nolock` flag.** Automated browsers can't take pointer lock; needed for verification.
- **2026-09-28 · Features add only the code they use.** Critic review of the scaffold (attempt 1) flagged speculative code; command fields, bindings and helpers now arrive with their features.
- **2026-09-28 · All level blocks collide as closed 12-triangle trimeshes with FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES, not cuboids.** Rapier 0.21's capsule controller sinks into any cuboid (any size) along its x = ±z diagonal planes; convex hulls and round cuboids too. Without FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES, face-diagonal edges give ghost ground contacts (wall sticking, crate climbing).
- **2026-09-28 · Kill-height safety net (`MapData.killY`).** Any character below it returns to its spawn with no momentum; defence in depth against physics glitches.
- **2026-09-28 · Ceiling detection uses a tolerance (`ceilingBlockFraction` 0.5).** Rapier trims upward moves by ~1e-5 m while sliding along walls; a strict check killed jumps next to cover.
- **2026-09-28 · Game keys only suppress browser defaults while playing.** Paused menus (slider arrows, Space on buttons) keep native behaviour.
- **2026-09-28 · Sun shadow frustum is fitted to the level's box in light space.** Guarantees every corner of the map gets shadows.
- **2026-09-28 · Per-chunk size budgets via a tiny build plugin (Rapier 4.5 MB, others 800 kB).** Vite has only one global limit, which Rapier's inlined WASM forces high.
- **2026-09-28 · Standing characters move horizontally only, then a ground probe places them 0.04 m above the floor; Rapier snap-to-ground is off.** Gravity in the main move made Rapier reject whole moves (~1 stall/s); snap-to-ground bumped 3.5 cm at triangle seams; resting at exactly the 0.02 m skin let moves graze the floor and lose up to 24% speed.
- **2026-09-28 · First map "Depot" is mirror-symmetric across x = 0 and roofless.** Mirror symmetry is the simplest fair layout for 3v3 elimination (each team's north lane is the same lane); a roof adds nothing to Phase 1 gameplay and would complicate lighting.
- **2026-09-28 · Cover heights: full ≥ 2.4 m, crouch cover exactly 1.2 m (crates, barriers, window sills).** Crouched eyes (1.09 m above the floor) hide behind it; standing eyes (1.66 m) see and shoot over it — a readable peek/duck rhythm. 1.0 m cover did not hide a crouched player.
- **2026-09-28 · Map layout rules are unit-tested: symmetry, cover heights (unclimbable, crouch cover hides), clear spawns, three connected lanes, no sightline between anyone within 5 m of either spawn, no straight lane line over 23 m, no sightline over 30 m between points on a 1 m grid (finer scans still find a few; see KNOWN_ISSUES).** Keeps fights at AEG/CQB range and maps honest as they change.
- **2026-09-28 · Only the ground probe decides "grounded" (it needs an upward-facing surface under a sphere narrower than the capsule).** Rapier's controller reports ground when the capsule's rounded bottom touches the top edge of cover, which let players hang on and hop over 1 m barriers.
- **2026-09-28 · Crouched hitbox top must stay ≤ 1.15 m above the floor (hitboxes arrive with the hit feature).** Crouch cover is 1.2 m; a taller crouched head would poke over cover to a standing shooter.
- **2026-09-28 · Prop tints come from a hash of each block's mirror-invariant position, from palettes without team blue/orange.** Mirror twins always match and prop colours never suggest a team side.
- **2026-09-28 · BBs fly at 88 m/s (AEG) and 72 m/s (pistol), slower than real replicas (~100 m/s).** Keeps BBs visible in flight (~0.45 s across 30 m) without making them easy to dodge.
- **2026-09-28 · Hop-up = Magnus lift ∝ speed × exponentially decaying spin; drag is quadratic.** Gives the airsoft arc: AEG within ~5 cm to 20 m and ~20 cm at 30 m, then clearly dropping; the pistol drops ~18 cm by 20 m. Tuning lives in config/ballistics.ts and config/replicas.ts; the shape is unit-tested.
- **2026-09-28 · An empty trigger pull clicks dry and starts a reload automatically.** Fun beats simulation; a manual R reload is still there.
- **2026-09-28 · Recoil is a small sim-side aim kick (0.18° AEG, 0.5° pistol per shot, recovers in ~0.1 s) and the camera shows it.** Replicas are toys: light kick, but the crosshair must stay honest about where BBs go.
- **2026-09-28 · Replica models: recognisable real-world silhouettes (AR-pattern AEG, polymer striker pistol), stylised rendering, two-tone (black + tan). Orange tips optional, off by default (VIEWMODEL.orangeTips).** User direction: guns should resemble real ones without AAA realism; toy feel comes from sound, recoil and BBs. Still no brand names, logos or copies of a specific trademarked design.
- **2026-09-28 · First-person hands with gloves and a team-colour armband on each sleeve.** Airsoft sites mark teams with tape; seeing your own team colour on your arm is readable and on-theme. Team colours live in config/teams.ts and are kept off neutral props (hue-distance test).
- **2026-09-28 · All sound is synthesised (Web Audio), no audio files.** Mechanical, plasticky replica sounds; BB impacts are positional and rate-limited.
- **Reload presentation:** the replica tilts about its own grip and the magazine (a separate part) slides out and back in; hands stay put until Phase 2 reload animations. Reason: keeps the replica on screen and shows a readable "mag change" cheaply.
- **Minimum on-screen size for BBs and impact puffs** (angular radius in BB_VISUALS / IMPACT_PUFFS). Reason: readability beats true scale (pillar 4); 6 mm BBs and small puffs vanish past ~10 m.
