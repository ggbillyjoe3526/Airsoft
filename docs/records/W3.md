## Decisions

- **A software WebGPU adapter is judged on the look bar's pixel share only (≤ 1 % of pixels over 24), not its mean.**
  Owner's ruling 2026-10-09, decision card in the graphics thread. Auto never draws with WebGPU on a software adapter, and the container's only WebGPU is
  SwiftShader: Depot's player views there score 0.51–0.63 (0.13–0.22 % of pixels over), from edges and ground grain,
  while the same twins score 0.12–0.22 on the node renderer's WebGL2 back end. Binds W4–W6's compare runs; a
  DECISIONS line is owed (this thread does not edit DECISIONS).
- **The nearest fire's shadow, other players' torches as real lights and the neon signs' coloured light stay out.**
  Owner's ruling 2026-10-09, decision card in the graphics thread: the look bar decides over AC2. Each was built and measured (KNOWN_ISSUES row).
- **Clustered lights are three/webgpu's own `ClusteredLightsNode` (32 px tiles × 24 depth slices, cap 64), subclassed
  as `NightLightsNode`.** 0.186 clusters point lights only; the subclass adds spot rows (your torch's real spot) and an
  allocation-free insertion sort, and skips the compute pass with no lights. Only real WebGPU clusters: the WebGL2
  back end has no storage buffers in a pixel shader, so it builds no extra lights.
- **Every lamp, sign lamp, fire and lantern within 16 m of the eye (to its pool's edge) gets its own light,** full to
  10 m and fading to 0 at 16 m, beside WebGL's fixed nearest-pool lights. 24 m failed Woodland Medium's overview
  (mean 0.92; 0.11 at 16 m). Shadows come only from the sun or moon.
- **Where WebGPU looks better than WebGL:** walls and players near any lamp within 16 m get real light, where WebGL
  lights the nearest 1–3: Neon Heights' side streets, warm light on players by Woodland's fires, the inside walls of
  Woodland's fort on Medium. Low lights as today.
- **Depot's baked light is bound per material.** Three 0.186 keeps a texture's old binding when a new one has the same
  version; `twinUniforms.ts` bumps it on a swap and `worldTwins.ts` binds the material's own bake.
- **Chrome 141's two rejections are fitted per device (`webgpuCompat.ts`).** Writing one layer of a drawable 3D
  texture (black frames) and Three's string `swizzle`; each probe runs once and a fit applies only where found.
- **Shader inventory (AC1).** `onBeforeCompile`: night sky stars (sprite twin; the moon drawn by Three as is), light
  fixtures (flames twin, embers sprite), dust motes and fireflies (sprites), set dressing ×2, the sky host and surface
  shader (surface twin), smoke plumes (smoke twin), figure finish (W3's twin, `figureNodes.ts`); surface materials
  without environment and the torch beams' 3 materials need none (Three draws them as they are). `ShaderMaterial`,
  all W4 and none drawn on the node path: temporal AA, light shafts, the post stack copy, reflections, lens, ambient
  occlusion, the post pass's full-screen material, the retro filter.
- **Look check:** 96 of 100 pairs pass (WebGL2 back end 50 of 50, worst 0.22; real WebGPU 46 of 50, Neon's aerial at
  0.43); W2's build fails 14 of the 28 new views (all 8 Neon night pairs pass on it: at night they cannot see a
  missing figure twin). Pictures and `scores.json` in `concepts/webgpu-w3-2026-10-09/`.
