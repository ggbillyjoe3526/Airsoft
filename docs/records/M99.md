## Decisions

- **Cause: a tile's picture filled the tile edge to edge under its words and the corner tick,** so the tick hid a rifle's muzzle and short tiles' words its grip.
- **Tile pictures sit in the tile's flow above the words, in their own 2:1 shape; the tick moves to the foot.** The same on the Loadout, Customise's colour tiles and the Armory.
- **`e2e/replicaPictures.spec.ts` maps each picture's drawn pixels onto the screen** and fails on any pixel cut by its slot or tile, or under the words or tick, from 1280×720 to 2560×1440.
