## Decisions

- **Practice's targets and timed challenge use Claude's defaults: 60 s runs, a 3 s countdown, two lit at a time, each for 3 s plus 0.04 s a metre, off by default, best kept per course.** The owner's note asked for an optional high-score run; these are easy to tune in `RANGE_CHALLENGE` (also in DECISIONS).
- **Every new target stands in an empty bearing from the spawn, so nothing hides another, moving or not.** A test samples the motion every 0.1 s for 12 s and fails if a target or post is in front of another.
- **Targets are placed from data with a facing and a floor (`RangeTargetSpec`, `RangeTarget.face`).** M94 places them on maps; the facing matches M94's own `face` {x, z}, so its merge drops its copy.
- **The START plate stands low (0.7 m) 5 m out in the middle, below every sightline, and only while the challenge is on, its stand included.** Shooting it starts the run without a menu, the way a range officer's start would.
- **A lit target is drawn through an orange "lit twin" instanced mesh, its own instance hidden.** No material swaps or new draw calls per lit target; the twins draw nothing while none is lit.
- **In a run, pop-ups lie down and rise only when lit; in free practice they stand and drop at random times (2.5–4.5 s up, 2–5 s down).** A lit pop-up must be worth chasing, and a pop-up that rises at random would score by luck.
- **The countdown is a synthesized shot-timer beep (3.2 kHz), and the run ends on the referee's long blast.** Range-day sounds, made in code like the rest.
- **A pop-up that stays down keeps lying (`keepDown`) instead of being knocked down again.** The critic's should-fix: otherwise lying pop-ups flicked upright for a frame and fell again when a run ended, the challenge went off or the range opened.
