## Decisions

- **On a software WebGPU adapter the Dev retro filter's views are judged by eye, not the look bar (owner, 2026-10-10,
  decision card).** On SwiftShader 14 of 16 retro views have 1.5–3.1 % of pixels over 24 (bar 1 %): scattered
  single-texel level flips on surfaces, where a sub-threshold shade difference crosses one of the six levels (a step of
  51). `nodeRetro.ts` takes WebGL's shader as is (the same 4×4 Bayer matrix, levels and texel rows), so no retro code
  change can pass it; on the WebGL2 back end every retro view passes. The owner's playtest on a real GPU is the check.
  Binds W5–W6's compare runs; a DECISIONS line is owed (this thread does not edit DECISIONS).
- **Every pass is three/webgpu's own node or a TSL port of WebGL's shader** (`src/render/webgpu/post/`): ambient
  occlusion, reflections, light shafts, temporal antialiasing, bloom, the finish (tone mapping, lens, grain) and the
  retro filter, behind one `NodePostStack` that runs WebGL's `PostChain` (split out of `postStack.ts`) so both paths
  order passes the same way. Where 0.186's own node draws differently from today's (its bloom and TAA), the pass is a
  TSL port of WebGL's shader, so the look bar holds.
- **Low draws straight to the canvas with tone mapping and haze in WebGL's order;** the late haze reuses one node and
  three uniforms per fog, so nothing is built per frame.
- **The held replica draws into its own multisampled target on the node path,** composited after the stack, for the
  same draw count as WebGL (a third MSAA buffer on Medium and up: KNOWN_ISSUES row, unmeasured).
- **Targets are freed through one `CompileGate` per renderer:** a resize, preset change, map change or lost device
  frees a pass's targets once compiles under way have settled (WebGPU errors on a pipeline freed mid-compile). After a
  lost device a free can wait forever if Three never settles its compile (KNOWN_ISSUES row).
- **WebGL's retro filter moved to a chunk of its own (`retroFilterWebGL.ts`),** keeping the main chunk at 949.0 of
  950 kB. A Dev look draws plain frames only until its chunk first arrives; after that turning it on is instant.
- **Look check:** WebGL2 back end 72 of 72 new views pass with equal draws on every pair, and W2's and W3's views still
  pass; SwiftShader WebGPU 57 of 72 on the full bar, 58 of 72 on the pixel-share bar W3's ruling sets for a software
  adapter (Neon Heights' Low ground view is at mean 0.51 with no pixel over), the 14 failing being the retro views above. W3's build fails 31 of the 40 new views. Pictures and `scores.json` in
  `concepts/webgpu-w4-2026-10-09/` (project files).
