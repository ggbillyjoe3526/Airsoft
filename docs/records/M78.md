## Decisions

- **Criterion 1 already held.** G2 and G5 split them: `replicaModels.ts` is 542 lines (with `replicaParts.ts` and
  `replicaBuilder.ts`), `config/render.ts` 9 (re-exporting five `render*` files), every part under 600.
- **High and Ultra draw plaster, cladding, asphalt, paving and glass at 512² (also in DECISIONS).** Glass is 512, not the
  audit's suggested 256: the owner's ruling names 512² for the flat city finishes. Ultra draws at 1024 like High, so it
  is capped too. Glazed tiles, with grout every 0.6 m, are not a flat finish and keep 1024.
- **Neon Heights High holds 21 MB less, not the criterion's ~28 MB (the owner accepted 21 MB, 2026-10-08).** Container perf run, 240 ticks: GPU memory
  112.36 → 91.39 MB, draw calls and triangles unchanged. Five 1024² maps with mipmaps are 5.6 MB each and 1.4 MB at
  512², so 21 MB is the whole saving; the audit's 28 MB was the five maps' full size.
- **The screenshot check: four 1920 × 1080 views of Neon Heights by day on High, before and after.** They match at play
  distances; pressed against a plaster wall its finest grain is a touch softer.
