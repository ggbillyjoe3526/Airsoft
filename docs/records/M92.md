## Decisions

- **3 holsters (1 primary, 2 secondary, rebindable); holstered sprint 6.8 m/s against 6.3; the draw out of the holster takes 1.8 times the replica's draw (rifle 0.81 s, pistol 0.54 s) against the ready sprint's 0.2 s raise.** The playtest note's trade, kept small: about 2 m on a 25 m dash for 0.36 s more on the rifle's draw (also in DECISIONS).
- **A slot key, a fresh trigger pull, aim press or reload press draws; a trigger or aim held as the replica went away doesn't.** Every "I want to shoot" press works, and holding fire through the holster key never snaps it straight back (also in DECISIONS).
- **Bots holster after 1.5 s of calm sprinting in their own half (nobody seen or heard for 5 s) and draw at once on a threat, in the enemy's half, on firing or reloading, or 0.5 s after the sprint ends (`BOT_BEHAVIOUR.holster*`).** The same command and rules as the player's; the gap keeps a turn on the route from drawing it (also in DECISIONS).
- **The holster's sound is a kit rustle the FoleyTracker plays from state, the draw keeps the `draw` event.** No new GameEvent (the contract stays PlayerCommand, by addition); the cue comes last on the title stream, so every other cue is sample for sample as before.
- **Third person shows the rifle slung across the back, arms at the sides (a seventh mesh per figure, one of the four drawn at once), and the aim rises from low over the second half of the draw.** No extra draw call; 0.36 MB of geometry a figure at High figure detail (64 kB on Low).
- **The four Medium Extraction perf baselines are re-recorded.** Woodland measured 40.36 MB of GPU memory against 36.65 MB on main (+10.1 %, the holstered pose; draw calls and triangles unchanged), Neon Heights 70.2 against 65.8 MB; within the 300 MB budget.
- **Bots' friendly-fire check uses the spread they shoot with now (`Character.spreadScale`), and a wall stops their BBs only if it also stands as far to either side as they stray by then.** The replica's own spread let a walking bot hit a teammate past a wall's end.
- **Balance: the holster leaves it as it was.** `balance.mjs depot` (24 figures): 0 outside, 2 outside within noise (Attack / Defend attackers 2v2 69.0 % and with ricochets 65.8 %, 16 seeds); on 48 seeds each, holstering on against off: ricochets 59.0 against 55.1 %, 2v2 60.0 against 61.4 %, 3v3 56.1 against 58.6 % (`pipeline/runs/M92/attempt-1/balance-holster-ab.md`).
