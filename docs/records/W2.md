## Decisions

- **Every world material gets a node twin chosen by program key, through a hook on the node renderer's material
  library.** The WebGL patches stay byte-for-byte as they are until W6 deletes them.
- **The twins read the GLSL patches' own uniform objects (`twinUniforms.ts`).** The two paths share one set of tuning
  and live values, so they cannot drift.
- **Twins with per-object uniforms refresh on every draw (`everyDraw`).** Three otherwise sent them once, which froze
  the flames, the plane and the neon flicker (QA); it costs 0 to 14 objects a frame and allocates nothing.
- **Sized points draw as instanced sprites on the node path.** The node renderer draws points 1 px wide; a sprite
  replaces the Points, so no draw is added.
- **The look bar: mean difference ≤ 0.5 and ≤ 1 % of pixels over 24 steps (`pipeline/webgpuCompare.mjs`).** It passes
  all 22 twin pairs (worst 0.084) and fails W1's plain materials on 7 of 11 views; the first bar (2.5) let 8 of 11
  through.
- **The compare hides figures, their contact shadows and torch beams, and waits a frame between renders.** The node
  renderer redraws shadows once a frame; without the wait the pairs differed run to run.
