## Decisions

- **The camo is printed per pixel from each part's own position, not baked per vertex.** The limbs' vertices sit
  about a blotch apart (~0.11 m), so the vertex print smeared into a faint shade. Three warped crossed waves with
  `fwidth`-softened edges (`figureCamo.ts`), tones 0.34 and 1.7 (`FIGURE.camo`), a seed per look; the WebGPU twin
  builds the same sums. Low carries no attribute and no cost.
- **The sleeves print the same camo at 0.3× scale on Hand detail High; the armband sits at 0.3 of the forearm.** At
  0.62 it fell below the screen edge.
- **A fresh contact's first aim goes wide sideways (tilt ≤ 30°) and settles from the trigger, not from first sight.**
  A first error up or down still hit legs or head, and settling during the reaction delay meant the opening burst was
  already on target. Hard starts at 0.6 m (was 0.25), Pro 0.5 m (was 0.2). Median first hit, before → after, 5 m /
  10 m: Normal 0.98 → 1.47 s / 1.58 → 1.71 s, none inside 0.75 s (was 33 % / 13 %); Hard 0.52 → 1.08 / 0.92 → 1.27 s;
  Pro 0.42 → 0.66 / 0.63 → 0.97 s; Easy 1.73 → 2.04 / 2.02 → 2.72 s (KNOWN_ISSUES row; reports in
  `pipeline/runs/G11/attempt-3/g11-measures/`).
- **Bots hold fire on a target with a teammate up to 25 m beyond it (was 15).** A deliberately wide BB hit a
  teammate 23.5 m down the line.
- **The movement faults the new pacing showed were fixed at the root, guards unchanged (coordinator's ruling):** a
  bot off its route leg rejoins it rather than walking over a ledge (Stack House stair side), a body on Depot's dock
  lip snaps to the dock (`NAV.snapDropShare`), a Follow me teammate routes its leader's way round a container.
- **With Ricochets count on, the playability guard allows bounced BBs up to 30 % of hits (was 25 %).** Wide first
  shots bounce: Attack / Defend 0.268, Elimination 0.232 (0.135 and 0.140 before). The setting is off by default. Put
  to the owner on 2026-10-09 (raise the cap, recommended, or weaken bounces); taken on the recommendation meanwhile.
- **Container perf baselines re-recorded (CORE-12):** matches play out differently. Low's peak draws do not rise
  (Depot Extraction peak 62 → 60, mean 34.5 → 38.0); Neon Extraction Medium reads 139.7 of 140.
