## Decisions

- **`Game.dispose()` stops its save watch and drops the audio-blocked callback; the menus stop their scroll watchers.**
  `watchScroll` returns its stop function. No test builds a whole `Game` or `Menus` (renderer, audio and DOM); the
  pieces they call are tested.
- **The two import cycles are broken by moving code, not by lazy imports.** Bot route following is `ai/routes.ts`
  (out of `botMovement.ts`) and the pool's table helpers `pool/tables.ts` (out of `caches.ts`).
- **The cycle check counts value imports only (`pipeline/cycles.mjs`).** `import type` is erased at build and a dynamic
  `import()` loads on demand, so neither is an edge; test files are left out. It runs in the fast suite.
- **Loop seams are bounded by percentiles of the loop's own interior (`audio/loopSeamSupport.ts`).** The jump and bend
  at the wrap under the 99.5th percentile of the loop's steps, the spectrum under the 99th of its adjacent windows; a
  click passed the old "half the biggest step" bound.
- **CORE-13 was done by TE2 (#147); CORE-14 here.** BP2's changelog lines now carry (#150).
