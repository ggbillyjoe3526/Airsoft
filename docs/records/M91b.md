- **Attempt 1:** the cause was found with `pipeline/webgpu-compare.mjs` and a local probe (not committed) that logged
  each shadow texture's create and destroy. On main, the torch's depth texture was made first by a material sampling it
  (the shadow map not drawn yet, `autoUpdate` false with the torch off), then destroyed and made again by the torch's
  first shadow draw while other draws still held it: 5 "Destroyed texture [ShadowDepthTexture] used in a submit"
  errors, and the torch view 33.6 % of pixels off the WebGL2 back end. After the fix: one create per shadow texture, no
  destroy, no errors; WebGPU against WebGL2 at night on High is 0.01 to 0.02 % of pixels apart on Woodland and 0.01 to
  0.03 % on Neon Heights, in all four figure views. Logs in `pipeline/runs/M91b/attempt-1/`.

## Decisions

- **The torch's shadow map is drawn once on its first frame, torch on or off, then only while the torch shines.** The node renderer then makes its depth texture once, in the draw, as it does for the sun's map; a frame's cost on High, once per match or quality change.
