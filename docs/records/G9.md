## Decisions

- **The dressing is look-only and lives in each map's data (`woodlandDressing.ts`, `neonHeightsDressing.ts`).** Colliders,
  nav, cover, bot sight and Neon's block order stay identical with it on and off, so no balance guard moves.
- **Woodland's horizon hills sit wholly beyond the east fence (nearest x = 70).** QA found the first east hill inside the
  field, standing over the fort and blocking all 25 spawn sight lines.
- **A plane pass starts every 20 s, start to start, at 26 m/s (a 10 s crossing).** The sky is empty half the time; the
  first build added the crossing to the gap (one every 36 s).
- **The three flickering signs share one slot schedule, at most 2 dips a second in all.** Each sign alone kept under 3,
  but together they reached 6.
- **Neon's skyline lights and the plane draw as part of the tree ring's mesh (`render/skyHost.ts`).** It brought Neon
  extraction Medium back under its 140 draw-call budget without raising it; Low, Woodland and Depot keep their meshes.
- **Signs mount flush on a solid face that backs the whole plate (`mountNeon`), or are dropped.** QA found six hanging
  off their walls.
- **Bark is olive-brown, not mauve.** Under the night moonlight the first colours read mauve; the new ones still pass
  the team-colour check.
