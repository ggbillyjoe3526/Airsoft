## Decisions

- **In Practice you change gear from play, and it is your one loadout** (also in DECISIONS). Picking up a replica in
  Practice is picking it for matches; a second, practice-only loadout would need its own save and a way to tell the two
  apart.
- **The Loadout key is L, rebindable (Settings › Controls › Loadout (in Practice)); in a match it does nothing,** as the
  loadout changes only between matches (owner, 2026-10-03). L was free.
- **The Loadout from Practice opens over its pause menu,** so Esc (Back) goes to the pause menu as it always did; Back
  to practice on the top bar, or the key again, goes straight back to practice. The top bar shows the key on that
  button only, with nothing along the bottom (playtest note 5).
- **It opens on the slot of the replica in your hands, and back in practice you hold that slot again** (`RangePose.slot`,
  `held`); a new replica there comes up with its draw, as after a switch. One in its holster (M92) stays there.
- **Unlock all gear needed no new sandbox:** the Loadout already lent every replica and part with it on and kept your
  own picks apart (`equip.dev.`). It now says so in a line, in place of the line sending you to the Armory.
- **Keys pressed on the menus no longer count as presses in play** (`Keyboard.endFrame` on resume), so the L that goes
  back doesn't open the Loadout again.
- **Practice's first line names the key** ("Practice · spare magazines refill · L for the Loadout"; Esc when unbound).
- **The M100 top bar test now expects the hidden Back to practice button;** what the bar shows outside Practice is
  unchanged.
- Back to practice rebuilds Practice where you stood, as the pause menu's Loadout did: 164 ms measured on the range,
  entering practice, where a loading moment is fine (owner, 2026-10-12).
- Merged with M93 (the pause menu under the Loadout carries the timed challenge switch; the first line names the key
  only while no challenge runs), M92 and M94 (the holster above; the first line says Practice). The holster lines came
  with the last merge, after the critic, with their own tests (`src/rangeSessionPose.test.ts`).
