## Decisions

- **Weaken bounces, not raise the cap (owner, 2026-10-10, decision card).** G11's wide first shots took the bounced
  share of hits with Ricochets count on to about 27 %; G11 had raised the playability cap to 0.30 meanwhile. The cap
  is back at 0.25 (`ai/depotMatchSupport.ts`). A DECISIONS line is owed under the ricochet ruling (this thread does
  not edit DECISIONS).
- **The lever is restitution: concrete 0.4 → 0.3, steel 0.55 → 0.41** (`BALLISTICS.ricochet`). On 48 seeds it took
  the share to about 0.20; `slide` 0.5 barely moved it and `minSpeed` 20 m/s only to 0.228. A head-on 80 m/s BB still
  leaves concrete at 24 m/s, so bounces stay visible. A knock-out speed rule would need code and would make counting
  ricochets that don't count.
- **Stack House's walk-off guard samples seeds 1–12, not 1–8, with the same floor of 2.** Main sat exactly on the
  floor; the guard is that every walk-off reaches a dead-zone spot, unchanged.
- The 16-seed share is noisy (a 0.40 vs 0.41 steel value moved Attack / Defend from 0.179 to 0.235); read about 0.20
  from the 48 seeds. Attackers now win about 60 % of ricochet-counting Attack / Defend rounds, inside the 35–65 % band.
