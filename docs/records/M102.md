## Decisions

- **Jump at a low obstacle vaults it, with no key of its own: 0.6 to 0.95 m over your floor and at most 0.95 m over the floor past it, at most 1 m deep, your full height clear over it, heading within 70° of your view; crouched, no vault.** One key that does what the obstacle asks; the Controls list reads "Jump / vault" and a prompt shows the key while a vault is there (also in DECISIONS).
- **A vault takes about 0.4 s: a rise onto the top eased over 0.16 s, then across at the speed you came in at (3.5 to 5 m/s), a push off down the far side; the replica is carried and fire held 0.3 s after it (the sprint's lockout).** Short and committed, quicker than walking round, never a way to shoot from the top (`config/vault.ts`).
- **Vaults are loud: bots hear one from 18 m (a sprint 16, a landing 12, a run 11), and its landing as well; the sound plays louder than a step.** The owner's ruling (2026-10-13) that a vault's speed costs noise (also in DECISIONS).
- **The maps' vault walls are 0.9 m, at most 0.4 m deep, open above: Depot's office hall, Woodland's cabin west wall and the Capsules' front on Neon Heights, each saving more than 5 m of walking; a sandbag wall on the range for the tutorial.** At 0.9 m a plain jump rides over about one time in thirty, and a wall thinner than a body is never somewhere to stand (also in DECISIONS).
- **Bots plan vault links on the nav grid (`nav/vaultLinks.ts`, the same `planVault` as the player) and take one only where it saves more than 3 m on foot (`BOT_BEHAVIOUR.vaultPrice`); a bot stuck on a vault leg plans without vaults for 6 s.** Vaulting is slower than its length and loud, so a short cut has to be worth it.
- **Through a vault the view tips down 4° and rolls 3°, eased in and out (less with reduced motion), the body tucks to 0.6 of a crouch and the replica is carried as in a sprint; the figures (behind the Dev switches) play the jump tucked by the crouch, the walk overlay off.** It reads as going over something without a lurch, and the figures' pose is a placeholder until the figure redesign makes the clip (`docs/HANDOFF.md`).
- **The vault sound is one new cue (two palm slaps, kit and a swish, under 0.15 s), last on the title stream after the holster's; the title sound budget goes from 1.4e6 to 1.5e6 samples (1,428,901 with both).** Every earlier cue renders sample for sample as before, and each still fits one spare moment.
- **Bots walking onto a lean spot or a guard post coast onto it and count as there once still, within 3 cm (`leanSpotReach`, was 0.1).** Found when the merge with M92 failed Woodland's guard test: guards came to rest 7 to 9 cm past their posts and some lean posts then saw their way in on 64 % of their ticks; over 12 seeds on each map every guard now sees it on at least 92 % (Woodland), 98 % (Depot) and 93 % (Neon Heights).
- **`ui/bugReport.test.ts` loads a fresh copy of the module (`vi.resetModules`), and resets again when it ends.** Its two copy tests failed whenever a file that loads the real bug report (`devSettings`, `crashScreen`) ran before it in the same worker (the shared module cache), seen on M92's gate and this one; reproduced in 3 of 4 shuffled orders, and none after (the KNOWN_ISSUES row is deleted).
- **A vault needs the top within 0.95 m of the floor past it too (was 1.15), the Knoll log lies across the slope, and a map check holds every vault link on Depot, Woodland and Neon Heights to that map's vault wall.** The critic's find: the log, its top level down a 1:2.5 slope, stood 0.6 m over its high end, low from both sides there; across the slope it is crouch cover all along (one bush beside it moved, which the map's generator no longer fits there).
- **Balance: no figure outside its band; Neon Heights' Pro Elimination leans east a little, left for the owner's playtest.** `balance.mjs` (92 figures, attempt 1): 0 outside, 1 outside within noise (the 5 m Hard duel's quick first hits, 15.8 % against ≤ 15 %, untouched by vaulting), 7 near an edge.
