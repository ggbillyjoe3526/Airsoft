## Decisions

- **The Ambience slider sits under Effects.** The beds and calls run through a per-match gain the slider sets, so the muffling and echo of the field still shape them, and Effects turns them down too.
- **The yard's new calls (crow, dog) are title cues.** Depot and the range render nothing extra at Play; a road traffic bed was tried and dropped for the same reason.
- **A distant dog, not a fence rattle, on the yard.** Nothing in the ambience should sound like a BB tick or a footstep.
- **Calls stay rare, low and far.** Gains 0.16 to 0.24 (birds 0.35), gaps of 14 s or more, 10 to 60 m out; Woodland and Neon Heights cues render three variants each.
