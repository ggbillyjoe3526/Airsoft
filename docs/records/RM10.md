## Decisions

- **Every robot wears the old sensor head made over with a visor and a headset with square headphones; the team
  shoulders sit flush with the arm, no plus marks, boxier and more mechanical.** The owner's two reviews of 2026-10-13,
  approved on the second round of pictures (also in DECISIONS).
- **Built headless in the container (pip `bpy` 5.0, `tools/blender/bot-figure/headless.py robot`), never in the
  owner's Blender.** The human thread held his Blender, and a cloud build needs nothing from his PC.
- **The rest-on-cover and vault clips are one layer both figures share (`bot18.py`), and the robot's mesh is its own
  (`bot19.py`).** The skeleton is the same, so one set of clips fits both and the human could take them unchanged.
- **The human's clips are spliced into its approved RM9 export (`splice.mjs`, which now adds a clip the base lacks),
  not rebuilt.** A headless Blender 5.0 build doesn't reproduce his Blender 5.2 meshes to the bit; the spliced clips
  match a build's to about 0.000001.
- **The Vault is an optional whole-body leg clip played by the vault's progress: 40 % of it on the rise
  (`FIGURE_RIG.vault.top`), the rest across the wall, blending in and out at 0.03 s (`FIGURE_RIG.blend.vault`).** The
  rise is short and the body must be at the top as the hips clear the wall; a slower blend lags the sim's crouch.
- **The match's hit-volume legs are the human's, any clip its file lacks taken from the robot's
  (`legsWithSpares`).** One leg table serves both figures, and a figure file without a clip still has legs that follow
  the move.
- **The vaulting hit-volume limits are one pair for both figures: missed 5.6 %, thin air 8.6 % (rising 9.8 % and
  11.5 %).** Measured 2026-10-13 on the v12 figures, a little over the worst of either, as the other rows are.

- **Five doc lines the critic found out of date after the splice are fixed in the records push.** Its check 8
  (docs current): the clip counts, the vault's spare legs, the vault rise's missed share and the robot's height.
