## Decisions

- **Practice's place is its own pick, saved as `practicePlace`, shown on the Map cards while Practice is picked.** Practising never changes the match's map; Day or Night is the map's own saved light, shared with its matches (also in DECISIONS).
- **On a map the targets stand where the other team's bots would play, from the map's data, in this order: lane points (facing back along the lane), the other team's starts and Extraction's home starts (facing your start), the overlooks (facing what they watch), then points every 9 m along a lane's long legs.** Bots stop, start and watch there; a new map needs no extra work. At most 48, the surest first (also in DECISIONS).
- **You start where Alpha starts round 1 (end 0's first spawn), and no target stands within 9 m of any of your end's starts.** The spawn yard stays clear, as a match's does.
- **Two targets on the same floor stand at least 3.5 m apart; floors 2 m apart or more are different storeys.** Neon Heights' balconies keep their own targets over the street.
- **A target never faces a wall: blocked within 1.6 m at head height, it faces your start, else the open way nearest that (16 ways round), else it is left out.** A plate rings only from its face side.
- **A figure with cover right in front at waist height (0.7 m) and none at head height crouches behind it.** As a bot behind a crate does.
- **Each spot gets one of the range's kinds (M93's) by why a bot is there: behind low cover a crouched pop-up and a crouched figure in turn; at an overlook a pop-up; on a lane's leg a slider where its track fits; the rest stand, every third near to far as steel (a plate, a small plate and a swinger in turn, the swinger where its frame fits) and the figures among them still, pop-up, still, slider in turn (a pop-up where the track doesn't fit).** Half the figures move, as on the range. Depot 17 targets (6 figures, 4 pop-ups, 1 slider, 5 steel, 1 crouched pop-up), Woodland 33 (4 sliders, 2 swingers), Neon Heights 39 (10 crouched, 2 sliders, 2 swingers); `src/map/practiceLayout.test.ts` checks each slider's track and swinger's frame against the map.
- **A slider fits where the floor at both ends of its 1.6 m half-track is within 0.15 m of the middle's and nothing stands across its face out to 0.4 m past either end, at 0.3, 1 and 1.6 m up; a swinger where the floor under both legs is level and nothing is across its swing or within 0.3 m over its pivot.** The gear (rails, frame) must not stand in a wall or hang off a ledge.
- **Every map has its own timed challenge best (its course is the map's id), and its START plate stands 3 to 6 m in front of where you start, at most 60° either side of the spawn's way, on a floor within 1 m of yours and in plain sight; you start facing it.** Neon Heights' spawn faces a wall 2 m away, so its plate stands 60° round and you face that way. Where no place fits, the map has no challenge (the pause menu doesn't offer it).
- **On a map a run lights only targets in your sight (a clear line from your eye to the target's middle) within 50 m, and the readout says to move up: targets light orange as you see them.** From the start almost nothing is in sight (Depot 0, Woodland 1, Neon Heights 0), so a run is played moving up through the map; a target out of sight or out of a replica's reach can't be hit in its lit time. With none in sight the run looks again every 0.25 s, not every tick (a ray per target each look).
- **The readout gives a map target's distance from where you stand when hit; the range keeps its marked distances.** A map has no firing line.
- **On a map Practice shows the minimap (no teammates, no one heard) and your torch beam at night; the range has neither.** As in a match on that map.
- **Practice compiles its shaders as it is built, as a match does (`Renderer.warmShaders`).** A map's worth of materials would otherwise compile in its first frames.
- **The range's card has its own picture, `range-day.jpg`, drawn by `pipeline/map-stills.mjs` from Practice (the street view: where you first stand).** The default place shouldn't be the one card without a picture.
- **The range is "Range" on its card and in the pause menu's "Practice · Range"; the tutorial pop-up's button reads "Practice".** Acceptance 1.
