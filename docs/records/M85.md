## Decisions

- **Every body stops a BB; only Ghosts and the shooter's own BB before its first bounce pass through.** A BB on someone already out, a teammate with friendly fire off, or anyone in a cease-fire stops there with no hit.
- **A BB stopped on a body that can't be knocked out knocks on it.** `bbImpact` names the body (`bodyId`): a plain puff and the body knock, never the floor's dust.
- **The hit volume is a head sphere and nine capsules that lean, crouch and swing with the drawn walk.** Sizes live in `config/hits.ts`; past a 2 cm rim at most 3.5% of the drawn body is missed and 2.7% of the volume is thin air on any pose.
- **Robots are drawn inside the human hit volume; the simulation stays look-blind.** Hits are the same whatever Settings › Look says.
- **Bots' tracking aim error rises by 11/9 on every level.** The wider shoulders made a strafing guard too easy to hit; the levels keep their order (Easy to Pro).
- **Bots hold fire while a body is in their line.** They no longer shoot through a teammate or someone already out.
