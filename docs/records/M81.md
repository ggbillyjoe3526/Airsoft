## Decisions

- **The range has a probe bake (`map/bakes/range.probes.b64`, 26 KB) and Depot's look on every preset.** `rangeSession.ts` reads it through `mapLookOf` as the matches do, and the combat effects get the map group for their light.
- **Props stand behind the firing line, in the corners and along the back wall; a sandbag berm lies at the backstop's foot.** Solid blocks like any map's, clear of the start, the walk to the line and every lane.
- **The dressing uses seed 2113, dirt 0.5, junk 0.3, litter 0.2, three puddles, wall marks 0.3 and no logos.** Enough for a used site without crowding the boards.
- **Glow strips are teal and lime, by the firing line only.** Clear of the teams' blue and orange; downrange stays readable.
- **The skyline has hills, treelines, site huts, a water tower and a power line; no containers.** Containers on the far hill looked like they floated.
- **`MapData.keepClear`: boxes the decals and wall marks never touch.** Before M81 the site roundel sat over the 30 m board; the range keeps a box round each distance board (`rangeBoardsClear`).
