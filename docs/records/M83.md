## Decisions

- **Skip to next round is offered on the pause menu, with a hint on the spectating line.** The spectator camera has no menu of its own; Esc is where the player already looks.
- **A skip runs the same ticks as watched play and shows only the round's end and the next start.** The result is the one the player would have seen, with no feed or sound spam.
- **With Disable Armory on, the tutorial drops its Armory line** (`tutorialSteps(armory)`).
