## Decisions

- **A rest needs the aim down sights, standing still (below `accuracy.stillBelow`), on the ground and leaned no more than 0.2, with a flat top 8 to 70 cm below the eye straight ahead, its face within 0.8 m of the body's centre and the aim clear 0.5 m past it.** Standing that is 0.96 to 1.58 m cover (the 1.2 m crouch cover, sandbags, sills), crouched 0.39 to 1.01 m (also in DECISIONS).
- **Rested, the spread takes ×0.65 on top of the stance and each shot kicks half as much (`MOVEMENT.rest.spread`, `kick`); it settles in 0.15 s and lets go in 0.1 s.** As steady again as crouching; the AEG's climb goes from 0.38° to 0.19° a shot, still visible (also in DECISIONS).
- **Tops steeper than about 18° are no ledge (`minFlat` 0.95, from the surface normal).** Depot's ramps and stairs gave false crouched rests without it.
- **No setting.** No stance mechanic (crouch, lean, aimed spread) has an on/off switch; resting only helps and lets go on any move.
- **The "Rested" tag sits under the sight from half settled (`HUD.restedFrom`), a small HUD panel in the HUD's capitals.** It is the one cue that shows at a glance whether the rest took.
- **First person: a 1.2 cm dip over 0.22 s as it settles, the sway ×0.2 and the kick ×0.5 while rested (`VIEWMODEL.rest`).** The pose change reads without moving the sight off the target.
- **The settle sound reuses the pouch's dull pat (`REST_SOUND`, 'reloadRefused') at the kit's foley level, played when the rest starts.** A new cue at the end of the title stream would have passed the 1.4 million sample budget (1,416,086).
- **The rigged figures (Dev switches) lean their upper body about 5° forward over the cover with the aim held on the shot (`FIGURE_RIG.rest.lean` 0.2); the built-in figures and your own body don't change.** A placeholder until the figure redesign (`docs/HANDOFF.md`).
- **Bots don't rest.** They follow the same rule through the same command, but they fire from the hip (`cmd.aim` false); having them aim when they peek over cover is a balance change for the bot personalities item (Dev 6, item 21).
- **Measured on every walkable point of each map, at 16 headings, standing and crouched: Depot 1,622 standing and 41 crouched rests, Woodland 2,922 and 563, Neon Heights 4,072 and 1,090 (`sim/coverRestMaps.test.ts`).** Depot's are its crates, barriers, sandbags, generators, IBCs, skips and 1.2 m walls; Woodland's its logs and boulders; Neon Heights' its barriers, crates, planters and low walls on every floor.
- **The tutorial's smoke step waits up to 30 s for the coach.** It failed 2 of 5 runs on main alone in the container (the range still loading after 5 s).
