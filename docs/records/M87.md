## Decisions

- **Hunting bots check by eye:** each glances at one spot a tick across its view (`perception.ts` `seesSpot`: within 19 m, in view, no further than the dark allows, never into a bush). A spot seen empty counts as checked for the whole team.
- **A bot whose hunt spot its team has checked turns to another before it gets there;** a spot it can see from where it picks is not picked.
- **A check holds for the level's `huntMemory` (Easy 20 s, Normal 40, Hard 50, Pro 60);** older, it counts as never checked, so the middle keeps drawing the hunt.
- **19 m, not 18:** at 18 the spacing and ricochet guards failed on their fixed seeds.
- **A bot at a lean spot walks aside while a teammate moving faster than `stuckSpeed` passes within `separationDistance`, then steps back** (`botMovement.ts`). A teammate standing close doesn't count, so two bots holding spots side by side keep their angles. Overlap on Depot seeds 1–20 went from 0.27 % to 0.15 %.

- **Balance is judged at the task's bands (35–65 % below Pro, LEVELS_BAND), not the M73 45–55 % line,** which came from Neon Heights and no Woodland band in code uses; the owner is asked to confirm.
