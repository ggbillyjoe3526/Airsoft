## Decisions

- **The beam is soft on every preset, Low included.** The playtest note is about the beam's look; Low keeps what the owner's ruling protects (no real light, no shadow, its 60 fps), and the beam's draw cost is unchanged.
- **The beam fades by the angle it is seen at and by distance from the lens.** `pow(|n·v|, coneEdge)` melts the edges and a smoothstep over `coneNear` 0.4–2.5 m hides the bright wedge at the muzzle; a cone fade-in of 0.15 softens its start.
- **The lit spot is a soft disc: a core the beam's width inside a spill (`spotSpill` 0.35 rad) over 6 rings.** It reads as a torch's hotspot and halo rather than a hard circle.
- **Shadows only from your own torch's real spot, on High and Ultra (`torchShadows`, its own Graphics row, needs Shadows).** Other torches are not real lights (W3); one shadow-casting spot keeps Medium's clustered lights and Low untouched.
- **The shadow map (1024) renders only while your torch is on.** `shadow.autoUpdate` follows the torch, so a torch left off costs nothing.
- **Shadow bias -0.003, normal bias 0.05.** Measured on Woodland at night: -0.0004 shadowed everything the torch lit and -0.002 still shadowed the grazing ground; -0.003 clears both and keeps a bot's shadow on a trunk.
- **Your torch's real spot starts at the rail, 0.16 m right and 0.1 m down of the eye.** Shadows from the eye would hide behind what casts them; from the rail they show, a little off the aim.
- **The beam's node material is a twin (`beamTwin`, `render/webgpu/`).** `torchBeams.ts` keeps a plain material so only `render/webgpu/` imports the node libraries (W6a's rule).
