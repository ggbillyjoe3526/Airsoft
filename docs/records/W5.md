## Decisions

- **The defaults held; none changed.**
  - The grass and stand-ins draw on the node path only, at Medium and up, with counts capped per preset.
  - Low and the WebGL path draw exactly as before.
  - BB flight stays in the simulation.
  - The compare runs with the grass and stand-ins off, through the page switch `?noGpuDressing` (DEV and e2e builds
    only).
- **Particles.**
  - Every pool moves by compute on the node path: dust motes, fireflies, chimney smoke and vent steam, impact, hit
    and gas puffs, rings, kicked dust, impact grit (`render/webgpu/compute/particleTwins.ts`).
  - Each CPU module hands its frame and spawns to a driver and skips its per-particle loop.
  - Each formula is written once in `particleKernels.ts` over `kernelOps.ts`: numbers for the tests, TSL for the GPU.
    Unit tests pin each formula against its CPU module, so the two can't drift.
  - A pool with nothing alive draws and dispatches nothing. The frame's passes go out in one reused batch.
- **Grass** (`grassField.ts`, `grassLayout.ts`).
  - A camera-centred clipmap of blade slots: a full square plus rings, each with twice the spacing of the one
    inside.
  - Density thins by four per ring with a hashed threshold, so blades shrink rather than pop. The last ring fades
    out.
  - Jitter hashes the world lattice, so blades don't swim as the camera moves.
  - Where grass grows comes from the ground grid, with blocks and puddles cleared.
  - Height and colour come from the terrain's own triangles, through a float texture.
  - Wind sway stops under Reduced motion.
  - Caps:

    | Preset | Slots | Reach |
    |---|---|---|
    | Medium | 41,952 | 76 m |
    | High | 93,696 | 133 m |
    | Ultra | 159,892 | 131 m (the concept's 160,000) |
- **Stand-ins** (`forestLayout.ts`, `forestAtlas.ts`, `forestStandIns.ts`).
  - Up to 2,000 trees from 12 m beyond the fence (behind the treeline) out to 150 m, at least 2.2 m apart, on the
    skyline's hills. Medium stands 900, High 1,400, Ultra 2,000; each smaller set is a prefix of the larger.
  - They are pictured once per map load from `atmosphere.ts` `detailedTree`, which was extracted for this and
    painted in the treeline's dark greens: 6 variants × 8 views, colour and normal.
  - Each is a cylindrical billboard blending two views, lit from the normal atlas, with alpha test and fog.
  - The map asks for both through data (`dressing.grass`, `dressing.forest`; Woodland only today).
- **GPU culling.** Both are culled per frame against the frustum, and the stand-ins also by distance.
  - On WebGPU, kept instances are packed with `atomicAdd` into an `IndirectStorageBufferAttribute` and drawn with
    `geometry.setIndirect`.
  - Three 0.186's WebGL2 back end runs compute as transform feedback, which has no atomics or indirect draws. There,
    the same pass sizes a dropped instance to nothing (KNOWN_ISSUES row).
  - The existing map dressing is merged static meshes, always in view, so nothing there was left to cull.
- **What 0.186 lacked, and the closest equivalent:**
  - No atomics or indirect draws on WebGL2 (above).
  - No cast from a select node to `uint`: multiply in float, then `.toUint()`.
  - A `positionNode` that gives world positions needs `receivedShadowPositionNode`, or the grass shades dark.
  - **A real 0.186 bug:** on the WebGL2 back end a draw keeps the vertex array bound to the first of a compute
    pass's two transform-feedback buffers. Every compute output reached the screen only every other frame: steam,
    flies and grass at half rate, and the grass culled for the frame before.
    `computeKit.ts` `followSwappedBuffers` drops that cached array for such draws. It hooks private API (KNOWN_ISSUES
    row), and a unit test plus the e2e steam check pin it.
- **Particle quads face the camera that draws them** (`cameraWorldMatrix` in TSL) rather than the camera at update
  time. In play the two are the same camera, so the picture is unchanged. Probes that move the camera after the
  update (the e2e steam check) now see the steam face them.
- **Woodland's Ultra triangle line stays at 250k for both back ends** (critic, attempt 1: a shared 700k line would
  loosen WebGL's guard 4.6× without the owner's word).
  - Budgets aren't split per back end, and only WebGL runs are judged.
  - The node path's count in a real Woodland Ultra match (WebGL2 back end, container) is about 637k, max 642,621. It
    counts every blade slot. A KNOWN_ISSUES row records it as unguarded until W6 splits budgets per back end and sets
    the node line from the desktop run.
  - WebGL in the same match measures 151k (max 157k), within the line.
  - DECISIONS line owed, for the planning thread to add: "Woodland's Ultra triangle line stays 250k on both back ends
    until W6 splits perf budgets per back end. The node path's container count (~637k with the GPU grass at its cap)
    is recorded unguarded in KNOWN_ISSUES (W5, critic attempt 1)."
- **Critic fixes (attempt 1, 3f1266b).**
  - Three's private API: `freeBuffer` (`_attributes`, pinned on a real `WebGPURenderer`) and `followSwappedBuffers`
    (`get`, `draw`, `vaoCache`, the `vaoGPU` its draw caches, `switchBuffers`) each log one console error when a
    member they use is gone.
  - The grass sway follows the match's MotionScale (`CombatPresentation.setMotion` → `Renderer.motion` → node
    backend), with no DOM query per frame.
  - The pools register in a WeakMap (`render/gpuPools.ts`), not their objects' `userData`, so clone and toJSON meet
    no cycle.
- **Free.** The compute buffers, the grass and the atlas are freed on a preset change, a map change (the map group
  leaves the scene) and a lost device. Nothing is sized to the screen. The atlas's depth buffer is freed straight
  after the capture.

## Known issues left

- On the WebGL2 back end the grass and stand-ins draw every slot, with culled ones sized to nothing (KNOWN_ISSUES,
  document).
- On WebGPU the triangle counts show the indirect draws at their caps (KNOWN_ISSUES, document).
- The tree stand-ins are flat cards close up and cast no shadow (KNOWN_ISSUES, can wait).
- `freeBuffer` and `followSwappedBuffers` lean on Three's private API; each now says so loudly if an upgrade renames it
  (KNOWN_ISSUES, document).
- Woodland Ultra on the node path is over the 250k line, unguarded until W6 (KNOWN_ISSUES, document).
- Left out:
  - Grass casting shadows, and grass bending under players.
  - Occlusion (Hi-Z) culling: frustum and distance only.
  - Reading the true kept counts back from the GPU.
  - Grass on the WebGL path, by design.
