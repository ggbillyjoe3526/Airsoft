## Decisions

- **No squad cards, order line or key hints on screen; orders show only on the Z wheel.** The owner's ruling at the
  2026-10-08 screenshot review; the order notices still reach a screen reader through an off-screen status region.
- **HUD opacity is a setting, 50 to 100 %, default 90 % (saved as `hudOpacity`).** The owner asked for a more opaque
  HUD with a level he can set; only the panels fade, the text, team blocks and bars stay solid.
- **The round banner reads "ROUND 1" in heavy capitals on its own panel.** The owner's 2026-10-08 note; its timing is
  unchanged.
- **The score bar's half width is 365 px, with a clamp that keeps it clear of the minimap.** The widest team row fits
  at 1280 px.
