## Decisions

- **The West Rise, a low ridge across the meadow in front of the west camp, hides each start from the other:** nobody within 8 m of one camp's spawns sees anyone within 8 m of the other's, eye to head and chest.
- **The west camp stands 1 m up and the field east of the Knoll falls 0.75 m,** so the east end's edge at the start is about 1.5 m, not 3.5 m.
- **Maps get a least team size (`teamSize.min`):** Woodland is 5 to 6 with 5 standard; the Match screen's Team size row hides the rest; an Extraction run's squad ignores it. The content toolkit's map rules check `min ≤ standard ≤ max`.
- **Woodland's lanes stop at two points inside the fort, not three:** with a point at each baffle the east team sat in the fort about 8 s, and the downhill end won 67.7 % at Normal. Now 57.1 %. Taking out the fort-gap points too overshot to 39.3 %.
- **Woodland's perf baselines are re-recorded at 5v5** (Low Elimination 43.7 draw calls, was 36.7 at 4v4).
