## New
- Fire selector with single, burst and auto, faster reloads, a crouch toggle and steadier aim when you stand still.
- Aim down sights with its own sensitivity, and a red dot sight to fit.
- BB streaks from the muzzle, hop-up dials, and your loadout reachable from the pause screen.
- Loadout screen: choose primary, secondary and grenade slots, BB weight, hop-up, optics, grips and magazines, with a Customise view and a performance sheet for each replica.
- Replica tiers, an 11.1 V battery and a site energy limit.
- Tight-Bore and Long barrels for the AEG and a silencer for both replicas; bots hear silenced shots from half as far.
- Cyber Pistol: a rare Legendary electric pistol with semi, burst and auto and a sound of its own.
- Glowing BBs, set per replica to At Night, Always or Off.
- New title screen with New game, Mode, Difficulty, Loadout and Settings, and a Map pop-up.
- Custom matches: rounds to win, round time, team size, friendly fire and ricochets.
- Match rules: Skirmish, or Custom with overtime, time-outs, minimap, fire modes, magazines and kit.
- Practice range with steel and figure targets from 10 to 60 m.
- Tutorial with coached steps on the practice range, which you can skip or resume from the pause screen.
- Squad orders (Follow me, Hold here, Regroup) with an order wheel.
- Minimap with your teammates, enemies where you last heard them, and the floor you are on.
- Hit feed, teammate markers, a hold-Tab scoreboard, a match summary and personal records.
- A "What got you" card after you're hit, explaining where the shot came from.
- Depot rebuilt: an uneven layout, a raised dock, site props and one flagpole in the defenders' bay.
- Field Credits from matches and the Armory: buy Tokens and draw replicas and parts, with an Epic or better guaranteed within 20 Shots and a Legendary within 100.
- Save backup: download your save, load it back, and daily restore points.
- Graphics presets Low, Medium and High plus Custom, a frame-rate limit, an FPS counter, and a starting preset picked for your graphics card.
- A new look: sky, trees and clouds, dressed surfaces and props, detailed players, replicas and gloved hands, reflections and soft contact shadows.
- Line drawings on Loadout and Armory tiles.
- A dark loading screen with a progress bar, and an icon in the browser tab.
- Sound rework: every replica sounds different, sounds come from where they happen and are muffled through walls, and volume sliders.
- Outdoor ambience with distant birds, and each field has an echo of its own.
- Controls: a second key for every action, mouse wheel and mouse button binding, raw mouse input, cm/360 sensitivity, invert mouse, and aim and sprint toggles.
- Accessibility: colour-blind options, sound cues, high-contrast styles, reduced motion, screen-reader messages, HUD size and a custom crosshair.
- Field of view slider, fullscreen, and pausing when the tab is hidden.
- A crash screen with Reload and Copy Report.
- The title screen shows the build name, like "0.1 Dev 4".
- Dev content switch in Settings, Dev: shows the maps, modes and gear still being built. Their matches are not recorded or paid.
- In development: Woodland, a night forest with bushes that hide you, camp fires, lanterns and its own sounds.
- In development: Neon Heights, a three-floor neon city with a Sky Bridge, Day or Night, and its own sounds.
- In development: Extraction mode on all three maps: loot cases, get out alive, and face waves, guards, patrols and hunters, with Supply weekends and a Halloween night run.
- In development: Pro difficulty, with bots that aim, move and work together like skilled players.
- In development: Tournament and Pro CQB rules, 4v4 and 5v5, a weapon torch on T, and a Retro pixels look.
- The game's code is under the MIT licence.

## Changed
- BBs fly by real air physics with drag and spin that wears off, and a light breeze drifts them downwind.
- Bots keep apart, react to near misses, hold crouched positions, and play differently by difficulty; Easy bots react more slowly.
- Against Easy opponents your teammates play on Normal.
- Attack / Defend: one attacker raises the flag while the others guard it, and Depot's two ends are evened out.
- Field Credits pay only for rounds you took part in and scale with match length, and the summary says why a round paid nothing.
- The Armory asks before big spends and shows the rarest items first.
- With ricochets on, a bouncing BB can hit the player who fired it.
- Low runs faster, and High has crisper, steadier shadows.
- Settings, Graphics folds the Custom rows away until you pick Custom.
- Cleaner menus and HUD with one consistent style.
- Key names follow your keyboard layout.
- World sounds carry further, and getting hit briefly dips the rest of the mix.
- The pistol leans slightly left again.
- Playing the same map again is quicker, and Woodland and Neon Heights start sooner.
- The Armory odds caption says the odds are for each item drawn.

## Fixed
- Night ground, wood and faces are readable instead of near black.
- The countdown beep can be heard, distant sounds fade smoothly behind walls, and sounds stay in place when you look up or lean.
- Extraction announces the last minute and a late exit, and its Match pop-up no longer offers rounds.
- Cases no longer open through walls, and guards watch the way in.
- A save from a newer version of the game is never overwritten.
- When another tab saves first, the Armory reloads and tells you instead of undoing your Shot.
- A second tab always waits behind the "open in another tab" notice.
- The first Play, the first frame and the first flag pickup stutter less.
- Bars fill smoothly, and Key Bindings no longer holds on to the mouse wheel.
- Reduced motion follows your system setting, and High Contrast shows the case prompt, coach and range readout.
- Your left hand holds the rifle's handguard, and name tags sit just above heads.
- Bots' AEG shots are always heard.
- A jump pressed just before landing still happens, and a click right after sprinting fires as soon as it can.
- Stepping down a kerb no longer widens the crosshair, and drawn Elimination rounds replay.
- A hit player always reaches the dead zone.
- Burst and semi double-taps never fire early, and pallet racks soak up BBs instead of bouncing them.
- Shot spread is even when aiming steeply up or down, and bots lead moving targets correctly.
- Esc resumes from the pause screen, and the first mouse jump after locking is ignored.
- Pausing fades the sound instead of clicking, and you get a hint when the browser blocks audio.
- No birds sing at night.
- Turning Edge smoothing on or off no longer leaks graphics memory.
- The empty magazine hint names your reload key.
- Crash reports read properly, and maps in development download only when needed.
- Sounds use less memory.
