## New
- Play on a computer in your browser at https://ggbillyjoe3526.github.io/Airsoft/ with nothing to install: it always runs the latest release and keeps its own saves.
- New menus in a bold navy and orange look: a plain title screen with "Call your hit. Go again." and START, and every screen works with the mouse alone.
- Match screen: maps and modes as picture cards with Day or Night, the match rules and a Your match panel on one page, and Practice as the last mode.
- Two teams, Alpha in blue and Beta in orange, with a Your team pick on the Match screen.
- Loadout and Customise show your replicas and parts as pictures, and sort and filter them by rarity.
- The Armory's collection is a picture grid with each kind of gear in its own column; scrap spares from a right-click menu.
- Settings in tabs (Graphics, Display, Audio, Controls, Gameplay, Accessibility, Look, Save file, Dev) with a search box and a short note on every row.
- The HUD takes the menus' look: a new score bar, a framed minimap, a cleaner hit feed, a picture of the replica you carry, and a HUD opacity setting.
- Map cards, mode cards and the menu backdrop are redrawn to show the maps as they look now.
- GBB Pistol replaces the Gas Pistol with the same stats: its slide kicks back on every shot and locks back on an empty magazine until you reload.
- Iron sights on every replica: right click lines up the sights when no optic is fitted, and a red dot or scope sits on top.
- Aimed fire is twice as tight as hip fire, and holding Shift while aiming creeps even slower.
- Smaller, lower iron sights on the AEG Rifle, GBB Pistol and Cyber Pistol, and a lower top on the Cyber Pistol in place of its fin.
- Inspect key (Y): turn the replica in your hands side on to look it over; firing, reloading, sprinting or aiming stop it.
- Replicas rebuilt blockier and two-tone with stippled grips, a redesigned red dot and silencer, and the Cyber Pistol in white with a cyan glow.
- New players: masked humans with high-cut or bump helmets in team camo and a team-colour plate carrier, and robots in light or dark shells.
- Your hands wear dark gloves and sleeves in your team's camo with its armband, or robot arms when you play as a robot, and your left hand holds a fitted vertical grip.
- Settings, Look: Robots mixes humans and robots on both teams, Realistic colours keeps replicas plain, and the Retro pixel look is now for everyone.
- Ultra graphics preset for fast graphics cards, frame-rate limits up to 240, and new effects: bloom from Medium, shade in corners and light shafts on High, reflections and film grain on Ultra.
- The game draws with WebGPU where your browser has it and with WebGL elsewhere, looking the same on both; Settings, Graphics, Renderer lets you pick.
- The game loads faster: a smaller start-up file and a physics engine 0.3 MB lighter bring the title screen up sooner, and the Loadout, Armory, Settings and practice range load in the background once it shows.
- Depot by day: a lower, warmer sun with long shadows, light bouncing into the shade, and grey concrete walls and wire gabions in place of sand.
- Depot dressed on Medium and up: weathered walls, junk and litter, puddles, sprays and glow strips, sheds, a crane and smoking chimneys beyond the walls, and dust when you sprint or land.
- The practice range and tutorial in Depot's new look, with props behind the firing line, a sandbag berm and wooded hills beyond the walls.
- Torch beams at night are soft and diffused, and on High and Ultra your torch casts real shadows.
- More varied ambience: rare, distant sounds like a crow, a dog, pigeons, a car horn and a creak in the woods at night, with its own Ambience volume.
- Tutorial: the real scoreboard and order wheel, plain-word steps in your own keys, a Practice range or Menu choice at the end, and Replay tutorial in Settings.
- Hit marker setting: the red X when your BB lands is now Off by default; the hit tick sound stays.
- Skip to the next round from the spectator camera while you're out.
- Minimap setting: fixed with north up, or rotating.
- Bots hunt the middle of the map once their lane is clear, keep out of lantern light at night and switch their torch on only for close fights.
- Hunting bots check corners by eye, share what they have checked with their team, and step aside for a teammate walking past.
- Bounced BBs lose more speed, so with ricochets on fewer of them knock players out.
- More Dev settings for playtesting: Field Credits and Tokens on demand, free case openings, time of day, bots hold fire, spectate any bot, end the round and reset the save; Disable Armory now hides the Armory everywhere.
- Report a Bug in Settings, Dev, Diagnostics and on the crash screen: write a note and it opens a GitHub issue with your note and a fuller report filled in (recent errors, frame times, memory and the last game events).
- Blender models are still being tested and are off by default: in Settings, Dev, turn off "Disable all models" to see them, and turn off "Disable figure models" too for the players and bots.
- Blender models: a new AEG Rifle with a slotted handguard, a full-length top rail, a collapsible stock and a magazine with a BB window.
- Blender models: a new Cyber Pistol with a long white top, slanted vents and a pink core window; only its dark slide kicks back, and its fire selector slides between modes.
- Blender models: a new GBB Pistol with a slimmer slide flush with the frame, a shorter grip and low sights.
- Blender models: in first person the AEG's trigger, fire selector and magazine release move, and Inspect pulls the charging handle, opens the hop-up cover and checks the pistols.
- Blender models: a smaller red dot, 2× scope, silencers and torches that sit lower, with the aim following them.
- Blender models: real pictures of the batteries and gas bottles in the menus.
- Blender models: rigged human and robot players and bots that run, crouch, aim, reload, switch to the pistol and call their hits.
- Blender models: players and bots kneel and crouch-walk lower, so a crouched head stays under crouch cover, and their steps keep time with their speed.
- Blender models: BBs hit players' and bots' legs where they're drawn, and a peek leans the upper body from the hips together with its hit area.
- Blender models: the player and bot figure files are half the size, so they load faster.
- In development: Woodland dressed with leaf litter, logs, mud and puddles by the creek and the camps, moss and fireflies at night, dark conifers and hills beyond the fence, and swaying grass on WebGPU.
- In development: Woodland hides both teams behind cover at the start, its blue end loses some of its height edge, and it plays 5v5 by default or 6v6.
- In development: Neon Heights dressed with litter, puddles, posters, flickering neon signs and drifting steam, and a city of lit towers with a passing plane beyond the walls.
- In development: Extraction's home team grows with the difficulty, and a squad member back from respawn has 3 seconds' grace.

## Fixed
- BBs stop at the first body they hit, so a nearer bot no longer lets a BB through to the one behind, and hit areas match the figure in every pose.
- Bots no longer land their first hit almost at once up close: their first BBs go wide and walk in, while Hard and Pro stay quicker.
- Bots lead their shots by the replica in their hands, and with friendly fire off they no longer hold fire for a teammate standing beyond their target.
- Bots no longer ride up the side of a stair or walk off the loading dock, and a Follow me teammate goes the right way round containers.
- No more frame hitches when you're hit or a bot plans a route, and fewer small hitches from BB trails, dust and the first dust puff of a match.
- The team camo reads clearly on Medium and up, and your left arm wears its camo sleeve and armband.
- Your rifle's left thumb lies along the handguard instead of looking like a second barrel.
- Customise takes "No light" on every replica, and it stays after a reload.
- Replica pictures on the Loadout, Customise and Armory tiles show the whole replica, never cut off.
- Menus and the HUD fit 1280×720 and 1366×768 screens: the scoreboard, the round table, Start match, Continue, the hit feed and the minimap caption.
- The round message sits clear of the HIT! stamp and waits while you call your hit.
- Settings tabs no longer jump, map card names read over light pictures, and small HUD text is at least 15 px.
- Skip to next round works after the browser refuses the mouse and no longer carries an AEG's wind-down sound into the next round.
- Starting a match asks for the mouse first, so a slow start no longer fails with "The browser needs a moment before re-capturing the mouse".
- Long sessions no longer creep up in memory.
- If the graphics card resets and WebGPU can't restart, the game carries on with WebGL instead of stopping.
- On WebGPU, switching on your torch at night on High or Ultra no longer freezes or garbles the picture.
- On WebGPU, Chrome 141 no longer stops on the crash screen, and Depot's bounced light and shade show again.
- On WebGPU, lamp pools and the torch spot show under the Retro look, a match on Low starts with less of a hitch, and spectating on Medium no longer stalls.
- The title screen no longer freezes while it prepares surfaces at High.
- No ghost of the last view after a new round or a switch to another player while spectating.
- Spectating shows the watched player's lean.
- The passing plane fades in and out instead of blinking, and Neon Heights' blade signs stand out from their towers.
- Standing on a Woodland log sounds like wood, and on a boulder like stone.
- The Cyber Pistol shows its own shape on bots, and its torch no longer pokes into the trigger guard.
- The site roundel no longer covers the 30 m board on the practice range.
- Extraction's exit line wraps instead of running over the minimap, and its reserve player no longer shows as hit in the tables.
- Match settings only offer what fits the mode: no Tournament rules in Extraction, and no time-outs in Attack and Defend.
- A round ended from Dev settings stays out of your records.
- An old save whose fire or move key the browser now refuses gets its default key back.
- If the maps in development fail to download, the menus say so.
- Screen readers no longer hear a drawn round's countdown every second, and Reduced motion follows a system change until you pick one yourself.
- The game's code is under the MIT licence; the 0.1 Dev 4 notes named the wrong one.
