## New
- Practice has moving targets (sliding figures, a swinging plate, small plates at 15 m and pop-up figures) and a Timed challenge, off by default in the pause menu: 60 seconds on lit targets, with your best score kept.
- Practice on any map, by Day or Night, with targets standing where the bots would play instead of bots; the range is now simply called Practice, and the timed challenge keeps a best per map.
- Practice with any replica: L opens the Loadout from the range and brings you back where you stood with the new replica in your hands.
- Levels and XP replace the Armory's gacha: every match earns XP, you level up from 0 to 10 and each level unlocks parts for good. The Armory is now the levelling screen, showing your level, what comes next and every level's items; the match summary shows the XP you earned and any level-up. Field Credits, Tokens, cases and rarity are gone, Extraction cases hold XP, and an older save loads at level 0.
- Press 3 to holster your replica: holstered you sprint a little faster, but drawing out of the holster takes longer than raising from a sprint. Bots holster on calm sprints too.
- Vaulting: jump at a low, waist-high wall to go over it. Vaults are loud and bots hear them from further than a sprint; bots vault too, Depot, Woodland and Neon Heights each get a low wall that opens a new route, and the tutorial has a Vault step.
- Rest your replica on cover: aim still at a crate, low wall, sandbags or window sill for steadier aim with half the kick. Moving, reloading, leaning or looking away lets go.
- Five saved loadouts on the Loadout screen, switched with keys 1 to 5 or a click, with rename, duplicate and delete from a right-click menu; the Match screen lists them beside Change.
- Bots have names (Dash, Rook, Fox, Owl and others) and one of four personalities, shown on the scoreboard and the "What got you" card: Rushers push in, Anchors hold a spot with the replica rested, Flankers come round the side and Careful bots clear each corner. Personalities change how bots play, never how well they see or aim.
- Bots carry gear that fits the difficulty and their personality: Easy bots are mostly plain, each level up brings more optics, grips and lasers, Pro bots are never plain, and a Hard or Pro bot sometimes carries a Cyber Pistol. A bot with a scope aims differently from one with a red dot, silenced bots are heard from nearer, and their figures show what they carry. Your teammates keep plain replicas.
- Adaptive difficulty in Settings, Dev, off by default: the other team starts at your Opponents level and adapts to how you do.
- Bot teammates call what they see and do in a text feed at the bottom left with a radio click: "Contact, Dock!", "Reloading!". Map places have names (Dock, Bay, Fort, Atrium, Sky Bridge and more) and the minimap shows where you stand.
- Pings: middle mouse marks where you look for your team, with its distance, or marks an enemy you can see as a contact. Bot teammates go and check or watch it and answer in the callout feed, and a teammate calling "Contact" sometimes marks that enemy for you. Settings, HUD, Ping markers hides the rings.
- Ask a teammate for a magazine: low on BBs, press H or Need a Mag on the order wheel and the nearest bot teammate with a spare walks over and tosses you one. Bots ask you and each other too; H or Toss a Mag hands one over. Magazines only fit their own kind, and no BBs are made.
- Bushes stop BBs: one that goes deep into the leaves stops there, one clipping an edge flies on thrown off its line and slower, with a rustle and falling leaves. Firing from deep inside a bush says so, and bots don't fire into thick bush (Easy bots still do).
- Noisy props: running into skips, containers, vans, racks, crates and fences makes a noise bots hear, sprinting on Woodland's gravel or steel ramps and stairs carries further, and a BB pinging off steel makes an idle bot turn to look.
- Overtime flag in Elimination: when the clock runs out with nobody ahead, three whistles blow and a chequered flag rises near the middle; the first team to touch it in 30 seconds wins the round. Bots go for it by personality. The old "Overtime" rule is now called "Win by two".
- Settings, Gameplay, HUD, Ammo counter: Off hides the BB counts and shows each spare magazine only as full or hollow.
- From the Dev 5 playtest: shots are louder on every replica with a Power source volume slider for battery, gas and spring sounds; the hit marker is a bold red X with its own scale, thickness, outline and colour settings, still Off by default; the 2x scope works like the red dot with the replica in view; you can fire while sprinting, less accurately, and the short lockout after a sprint is gone; Esc while you are out skips the round at once.
- New human and robot figures when the Dev figure switches are off: humans with gloved hands, four heads and team colour on the vest and armbands, robots with a wraparound visor, team light line, headphones, antenna and boxier mechanical limbs. Both rest the replica on cover and vault in their own animations.
- Blender figures (still behind the Dev switches) have a holstered pose: the arms hang and pump when sprinting and the rifle rides slung on the back.
- Replicas and parts rebalanced without rarity: the Low-Cap Magazine gets two more magazines and the Long Barrel more energy and tighter groups; Hard and Pro bots lose their small tier bonus. Unlock all gear is now Disable levelling, a cheat with no XP and no records.

## Fixed
- Menu and HUD text stays inside its buttons and backgrounds at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, and the ammo count no longer overlaps the fire mode chip on the replica panel.
- Glowing BBs have no halo, and the 2x scope models have no gap between the glass and the body.
- Bots firing on the move hold fire when a teammate is down the line, so they no longer hit teammates past the end of a wall.
- Bots taking a lean spot or an Extraction guard post settle right on it, so their lean sees round the corner.
- Anchor bots sent hunting actually hunt instead of standing still, and no longer aim down sights while sidestepping.
- Vaulting: a trigger held over a wall no longer fires a second BB or draws a holstered replica, presses made mid-vault happen as you land, and being hit mid-vault leaves you where you land, not back through the wall.
- Sprinting holstered over the top of a steep stair no longer lifts you off the ground, and a quick tap of the holster key no longer makes the held replica jump.
- Neon Heights: the Plaza stair can be walked up without jumping and run down without a drop, and bots use it.
- Close teammates' name tags no longer sit on top of each other, the overtime banner fits its line, and the callout feed hides under the scoreboard.
- Ping, flag, exit and hold markers pinned to the screen's edge keep off the minimap, and two moved off it sit one under the other.
- Timed challenge: a run with Game speed or Bottomless magazines on is no longer kept as a best.
- The save summary before your first XP shows your starting kit, not "Items unlocked 0", and the Match loadout tip names the loadout it cuts short.
- Leaves knocked out of a bush land on the ground and lie there, even under full auto.
- A blank line in bots.md, callouts.md or bot-kits.md no longer drops every row after it; the game reports it.
- The tutorial's aiming card says you can fit a red dot or 2x scope on the Loadout, and Woodland starts a little quicker.
- Blender figures (Dev switches): no jolt on landing or on cover, no double magazine after a sprint or vault, no flicker at range, pistols sit in the hands, bots' BBs leave the muzzle, bots with two pistols show the one they hold, and AEG iron sights fold under an optic. If the figure models fail to download, the built-in figures are used instead of an error.
